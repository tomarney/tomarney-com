# Biography

I am a geochemist at the University of Southampton, based at the National Oceanography Centre Southampton. After a BSc in Geology at Van Mildert College, Durham, I worked for four years in the tech industry in London before returning to science in the form of a master's in Oceanography at the university of Southampton. That led into a PhD in palaeoclimate and geochemistry at Southampton along with the British Antarctic Survey and a [thesis](/research/thesis) titled "West Antarctic ice-rafted debris: Sources, signals and signatures of past ice sheet dynamics" with [Steve Bohaty](https://www.geow.uni-heidelberg.de/forschungsgruppen/bohaty/), [Claus-Dieter Hillenbrand](https://www.bas.ac.uk/profile/hilc/),and [Paul Wilson](https://www.southampton.ac.uk/people/5wync6).

After four years investigating the past extent of the West Antarctic Ice Sheet via marine sediments, I swapped the frozen South for the balmy tropics, where I now analyse the geochemistry of reef-forming corals as a postdoctoral Research Fellow on the ERC-funded [Microns2Reefs project](https://www.thefosterlab.org/microns2reefs), under Gavin Foster.
